On the water

Private boats on the Tagus, for two guests or two hundred.

Lisbon was built to be seen from the river. We find the boat that fits the day - a sailing yacht for a few friends, a catamaran for a birthday, a river boat with a bar and a dance floor for the whole company - and bring the crew, the food and the plan.

Why the river

The best view of Lisbon is from the water.

From the Tagus the whole city lines up for you: the Praça do Comércio arcades, Alfama climbing to the castle, the 25 de Abril bridge overhead, and Belém with its tower and its monument to the navigators. Every one of them was built to be seen from a ship.

For a group there is a second reason. On a boat everybody is in one place, sitting down, with a drink, and nobody is queuing for anything. It is the easiest few hours of a group day to run, and usually the one they talk about afterwards.

Boats leave from the marinas at Doca de Santo Amaro and Doca de Belém in Lisbon, and from Setúbal on the Sado for the dolphins. We confirm the meeting point when you book.

Ways to spend the time

An hour, an afternoon or a whole day.

  • Sunset on the Tagus. About two hours past the Praça do Comércio, under the bridge and along Belém, timed for the light.
  • Cascais and the coast. About four hours out past the Bugio lighthouse, to swim, paddleboard and have lunch at anchor off Cascais.
  • Arr&aacute;bida, Sesimbra and the dolphins of the Sado. A full day south from Setúbal: turquoise coves, a long lunch and the estuary's resident dolphins.
  • Celebrations. Birthdays, anniversaries, hen and stag parties and proposals, with the music and the food to match.
  • Company days. Team building on deck, a launch, a results party or a conference dinner on the water.
  • Take the helm. Anyone who wants to can steer for a while, and sailing lessons can be arranged for those who want to learn properly.

Land and water

Put a boat at the end of a day on land.

The river works best as the last chapter of a day we are already running. Sintra's palaces in the morning, lunch, and then down to Belém for the boat as the light goes gold. Or a Lisbon walking morning and an afternoon at anchor off Cascais.

We plan both halves together, so the coach meets the boat and the boat meets the dinner, and you have one person to call about all of it.

If the wind is too strong to sail safely, the skipper decides on the day, and we move the trip to another time. You will hear from us early, not at the marina.
