Our Story

Big groups, done properly.

Founded in Lisbon in 2008. We plan private days out for groups — seven people or eighty — with a licensed guide in every vehicle.

The House

What we actually do.

We began as a single guide and a small Mercedes parked outside Rossio station. Today the whole of what we do is large private groups and events — corporate days out of a Lisbon conference, wedding guests between a hotel and a quinta, family parties of twenty, delegations of eighty.

That is a deliberate choice, and a recent one. A party of four and a party of forty are not the same job done twice; one is a conversation in a car, the other is vehicles, licensed guides in each of them, timed palace tickets bought weeks ahead, and a restaurant that can seat everybody at once. Doing both well, at the same time, is how companies end up doing neither.

So smaller parties now go straight to a trusted independent guide who looks after them directly — no mark-up and no middleman — and we put everything we have into the days that need the planning.

We are members of APAVT, fully insured, and proudly local. Lisbon is our home; Portugal is the story we love most to tell.

Our Promise

Five quiet commitments.

  • Always private. Your group, your vehicle, nobody else in it.
  • Always a guide in every vehicle, and every one of them officially licensed.
  • Always punctual. Door-to-door, on the dot.
  • Always honest pricing. Tickets and lunch at cost, and we tell you where the price steps before you commit.
  • Always reachable. A real person, every day, around the clock during your trip.
